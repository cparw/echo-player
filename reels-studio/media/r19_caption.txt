GTA 6's first two trailer anthems are older than GTA itself.

TRAILER 1 (Dec 2023)
• Main song: Love Is a Long Road, Tom Petty (1989, Full Moon Fever)
• REPORTED: Spotify told the BBC streams rose 36,979% week on week
• ROCKSTAR SAYS: Rob Nelson called it "a real sort of mission statement"

TRAILER 2 (May 2025)
• Main song: Hot Together, The Pointer Sisters (1986)
• REPORTED: Spotify streams up 182,000% in the first two hours vs the same window a week earlier
• REPORTED: the car radio also plays Wang Chung, Tammy Wynette and Zenglen

EXTENDED LOOK (27 Aug 2026, Netflix)
• REPORTED: 14 songs spotted by fans and press in 26 minutes. Rockstar has not published a list
• REPORTED: Phil Collins' Against All Odds plays quietly in an elevator, about 21:34

LAUNCH TRAILER (15 Oct 2026)
• ROCKSTAR SAYS: brand new song Flower in a Hurricane, Justice with Tame Impala, SZA and King Gizzard and The Lizard Wizard. Justice also compose the game's original score
• REPORTED: No. 31 on the UK Official Singles Chart, plus a Billboard Hot 100 entry
• REPORTED: Justice's Waters of Nazareth was on GTA IV's radio

OUR MATH
• The first GTA came out in 1997. Both early anthems came first
• The Launch Trailer song hit the UK chart 3 weeks before the game
