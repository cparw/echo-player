I went back through Rockstar's trailers and looked past Jason and Lucia. This is the wildlife hiding in plain sight, and where every claim comes from.

SPOTTED IN ROCKSTAR'S OWN FOOTAGE
• Launch Trailer (15 Oct 2026): a green iguana crosses the road in the first 30 seconds, and the Welcome to The Keys sign shows a heron and a shark
• Launch Trailer: a man relaxing at home with a pet snake
• Trailer 2 (6 May 2025): a cat sits on top of the ATM in the corner store scene
• Extended Look promo on Rockstar's TikTok: two gulls brawling on a sidewalk
• Rockstar's GTA VI site describes an Ultimate Edition screenshot as "Jason and Lucia on the deck of the Squalo, with dolphins swimming nearby"

REPORTED
• Over 170 animal species: 60 kinds of birds, 30 plus land mammals, plus sharks, whales and jellyfish (Rockstar to Game Informer, 29 Sept 2026, via VGC and Kotaku)
• Nonnative animals are in on purpose, including monkeys and armadillos (Kotaku). Green iguanas are on the list too (GamesRadar)
• Animals come in Rare, Discoverable and Legendary tiers, similar to Red Dead Redemption (GamesRadar)
• The Extended Look also shows manatees and stingrays (Kotaku, GamesRadar)

REAL FLORIDA, CONFIRMED
• Green iguanas are not native to Florida. FWC lists them as a prohibited, invasive species, and cold snaps can stun them so they drop from trees (myfwc.com)
• January 2020: NWS Miami warned people that iguanas could fall from trees overnight (NPR)
• Florida's wild rhesus macaques trace back to about six monkeys a glass bottom boat operator released at Silver Springs in the 1930s (University of Florida IFAS)
