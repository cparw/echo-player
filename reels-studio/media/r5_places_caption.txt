Rockstar's own region text, side by side with real Florida. Every source below.

ROCKSTAR SAYS (official GTA VI site, rockstargames.com/VI)
• Vice City: "the VC Port, the cruise ship capital of the world"
• Leonida Keys: "some of the most beautiful and dangerous waters in all of America"
• Grassrivers: "The gators may be the most famous attraction, but there are far deadlier predators"
• Port Gellhorn: "This is Leonida's forgotten coast"
• Ambrosia: "The Allied Crystal sugar refinery provides the jobs", plus a screenshot of a field burning at sunset
• Mount Kalaga: "A national landmark up against the state's northern border"
• The tour: "destinations across the sunshine state"

REAL FLORIDA, CONFIRMED
• PortMiami's registered slogan: The Cruise Capital of the World® (Miami Dade County)
• NOAA: the Keys reef is North America's only coral barrier reef
• South Florida is the only place alligators and crocodiles coexist (National Park Service)
• "River of Grass" is from Marjory Stoneman Douglas's 1947 book, published the year Everglades National Park was dedicated (National Park Service)
• Florida is the largest US cane region, mostly south of Lake Okeechobee (USDA)
• Britton Hill, 345 ft, is the lowest state high point in the US, about 2 miles from Alabama (USGS data)

REPORTED
• "Florida's Forgotten Coast" is a trademark first used in 1992 by the Apalachicola Bay Chamber of Commerce (Wikipedia, citing USPTO)
• Glades growers burn cane before harvest. Residents call the ash "black snow" (AP, 2019)

OUR READING
Rockstar never says any region copies a real place. Pairing Grassrivers with the Everglades, Port Gellhorn with the Forgotten Coast and Ambrosia with sugar country is our interpretation.
