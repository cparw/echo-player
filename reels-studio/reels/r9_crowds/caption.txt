Stare at a stranger in GTA 6. His dog might chase you 🐕

In Edge's GTA 6 demo, a man took Jason's stare as a test, squared up and unclipped his dog's leash.
GTA 5 NPCs dropped what you bumped out of their hands and forgot about it. GTA 6 pedestrians carry traits: temper, aggression, what they've seen, how you treated them.
Listen in too close? They notice. Follow too close? They may get aggressive or bolt, Rob Nelson told IGN.
Rockstar built a whole team in Los Angeles just for the people on the street, and art directed every one of them by hand.
Over 600,000 animations vs 55,000 in GTA 5, per Rockstar. That's about 11x. Watch one per second and you'd need almost a week (our math).
