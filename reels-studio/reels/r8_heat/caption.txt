In GTA 6, the police remember your outfit 👀

Rockstar's Rob Nelson told IGN the cops know your face, your clothes, your car, and that you're a couple.
No witness, no alarm? Nelson says the crime never gets reported.
Sunglasses won't save you. Nelson put on glasses and a hat live in the demo. They still knew his outfit.
The fix: buy a new outfit, or grab a spare from your car's trunk. They know the car? Jump out. They know you're a couple? Split up. But if either of you gets arrested, it's Busted.
Rockstar's own footage shows six wanted stars (GTA 5 stopped at five), and the cops mostly stay off your minimap now.
