## Line by line

**L1** "GTA 6 was first planned across parts of two continents."
- Verdict: SUPPORTED. Label: REPORTED (Bloomberg).
- [GI] "It started as "Project Americas," covering parts of two continents" / "Rockstar told Bloomberg the game was code-named Project Americas and once meant to cover parts of North and South America."
- [NBC] "the studio initially envisioned an enormous open world stretching across large parts of North and South America."
- "First planned" is fine (NBC: "initially envisioned"). Note: GI also says "The first plan was to build GTA 6 alongside Red Dead Redemption 2", which is about timing, not scope; no conflict.

**L2** "Then Rockstar cut almost all of it. And what they did with the piece that was left is why this game feels so real."
- Verdict: SUPPORTED WITH A WORDING CHANGE. Label: REPORTED (cut) / opinion (second sentence).
- [GI] "It was later cut back to Miami and its surroundings, the area that became Vice City and the rest of Leonida."
- "Almost all of it" is our characterisation; no source gives a share. It is geographically true in spirit, but it reads as a stated fact. "Feels so real": nobody outside Rockstar has played it (launch 19 Nov), so claim what we can show: "looks".
- Rewrite: "Then Rockstar cut it right back. And what they did with the piece that was left is why this game looks so real."

**L3** "2015. Miami. Rockstar's Rob Nelson lands with senior staff, on the game's very first research trip."
- Verdict: SUPPORTED. Label: REPORTED (Bloomberg).
- [GI] "In 2015, Nelson and other senior staff flew to Miami on the game's first research trip."

**Chip** "ROB NELSON, ROCKSTAR NORTH"
- Verdict: SUPPORTED. Label: ROCKSTAR SAYS (title as given to Dazed).
- [DZ] "Rob Nelson, head of development and co-studio head at Rockstar North"; [NIGHTLY] "Rob Nelson, who oversees Rockstar North in Edinburgh with Aaron Garbut"; [GI] "the three heads of development Rob Nelson, Aaron Garbut and Phil Hooker".
- Optional fuller chip: "ROB NELSON, CO-STUDIO HEAD, ROCKSTAR NORTH". (This is his current title; sources don't give his 2015 title.)

**L4** "The code name: Project Americas. Because the plan covered large stretches of North and South America."
- Verdict: SUPPORTED. Label: REPORTED (Bloomberg).
- [GI] "The code name was Project Americas, because the plan was to feature large stretches of both North and South America."

**L5** "Then it shrank. To one city, and the land around it. Bloomberg, who reported it, doesn't say exactly why."
- Verdict: SUPPORTED (tiny grammar fix). Label: REPORTED (Bloomberg).
- [GI] "cut back to Miami and its surroundings" / "Bloomberg doesn't say when or why the scope shrank".
- [NBC] "abandoned in favor of a more focused setting centered on Vice City and its surrounding regions. The report doesn't clearly explain why Rockstar dropped the original vision."
- Grammar: "Bloomberg, which reported it, doesn't say why." ("exactly" can stay; NBC's "doesn't clearly explain" supports it.)

**L6** "And what survived is still big. Vice City alone is twice the size of Los Santos."
- Verdict: SUPPORTED. Label: ROCKSTAR SAYS (to the NYT).
- [STAR] "Vice City, its alluring interpretation of Miami, is twice the size of Los Santos, the version of Los Angeles in Grand Theft Auto V, according to Rockstar."
- Backup [DES]: Nelson to DavyJones: "Vice City alone is twice the size of Los Santos" (translated).

**L7** "But the research never stopped. Rockstar says a team has been embedded in Miami for years. Hanging out. Listening to stories."
- Verdict: SUPPORTED WITH A WORDING CHANGE. Label: REPORTED (Dazed) for the team; ROCKSTAR SAYS for the quote.
- [DZ] "various groups of Rockstar employees have visited Miami and its surrounding areas over the last decade, and there is a dedicated research team embedded in the city."
- Problems: (1) that sentence is Dazed's narration, not a Rockstar quote, so not "Rockstar says"; (2) "for years" is attached to the visits, not to the embedded team. No source says how long the team has been embedded.
- Rewrite: "But the research never stopped. Rockstar staff have visited Miami for a decade. Hanging out. Listening to stories." (alt keeping the team: "Dazed says a research team is embedded in Miami. Hanging out. Listening to stories.")
- On screen quote: SUPPORTED verbatim. [DZ] "We hang out, get a feeling for the place, listen to the stories, cross-reference that with books and journalism…" Close the card with an ellipsis ("…listen to the stories...") since the sentence continues; Dazed's own pull quote does this. Attribution fine; exact title: Rupert Humphries, senior vice president of narrative at Rockstar.

**L8** "They rode along with police. Visited racetracks and nightclubs. Interviewed people from every kind of neighbourhood, and hired some of them."
- Verdict: SUPPORTED WITH A WORDING CHANGE. Label: REPORTED (NYT) for the visits; ROCKSTAR SAYS (Nelson to NYT) for interviews and hiring.
- [STAR] "Depicting a wide cross-section of society meant on-the-ground research for Rockstar – visiting racetracks and nightclubs, riding along with police officers, meeting with owners of gun ranges. To make the game feel as authentic as possible, Nelson said, the studio interviewed people who were respected in all types of neighbourhoods; Rockstar hired some of them as well."
- Missing qualifier: "respected". Rewrite: "Interviewed respected people from every kind of neighbourhood, and hired some." The Nightly reprint cuts the Nelson sentence; use The Star as the source.

**L9** "So when the first trailer dropped, Florida recognised itself. A woman dancing on a moving car. An alligator pulled from a pool. Both were real clips first." On screen "REAL CLIP, MIAMI, 2017" / "REAL CLIP, FLORIDA, 2019".
- Verdict: SUPPORTED. Label: REPORTED (Know Your Meme, from IGN's comparison). Rockstar has not confirmed it.
- [KYM] "each one was inspired by a viral video filmed in Florida, a comparison montage compiled by IGN reveals." / "Woman twerking on top of a driving car in Miami, posted to X / Twitter by user @BillyCorben in March 2017" / "Trapper dragging a massive alligator out of a pool, reported by WPBF 25 News in April 2019".
- KYM gives no town for the alligator clip, so "FLORIDA" (not a city) is right. Tweet is dated 14 March 2017.

**L10** "It was watched ninety million times in a day. A world record." On screen 90,421,491 / VIEWS IN 24 HOURS / GUINNESS WORLD RECORD.
- Verdict: SUPPORTED WITH A WORDING CHANGE. Label: REPORTED (Guinness World Records).
- [GWR] "The most viewed videogame reveal on YouTube in 24 hours is Grand Theft Auto VI Trailer 1 with 90,421,491 views, as of 5 December 2023."
- Overstatement: it is a category record (game reveal, YouTube only), not "a world record" in general, and views are not people. "In a day" = 24 hours is fine.
- Rewrite: "Ninety million YouTube views in a day. A game reveal record." On screen add "MOST VIEWED GAME REVEAL, YOUTUBE" under GUINNESS WORLD RECORD; the number and "VIEWS IN 24 HOURS" are exact.

**L11** "One state took them eleven years. Imagine two continents." On screen "11 YEARS" (OUR MATH, 2015 to 2026).
- Verdict: SUPPORTED WITH A WORDING CHANGE. Label: OUR MATH.
- Math is right (2015 first trip to 2026 launch = 11 years). But it isn't 11 years of building: [GI] "The first plan was to build GTA 6 alongside Red Dead Redemption 2. As Red Dead grew, Nelson moved most of the company onto finishing it ... and GTA 6 slipped back several years." and "more than eight years of work". So "took them eleven years" overstates the work.
- Rewrite: "One state. Eleven years from first trip to launch. Imagine two continents." (shorter: "One state, eleven years after that first trip. Imagine two continents.") Card: "11 YEARS, FIRST TRIP TO LAUNCH" tagged OUR MATH (2015 to 2026).
- "Imagine two continents" is rhetoric, not a claim; fine.

## "One city and the land around it" vs "one state"
Both fair, for different moments. Bloomberg describes the cut as "Miami and its surroundings" ([GI]) / "Vice City and its surrounding regions" ([NBC]), so L5 matches the report. The finished setting is Leonida, a fictional state based on Florida ([DZ] "Leonida (based on Florida)"; [GI] "the area that became Vice City and the rest of Leonida"), so "one state" in L11 describes the final game. Keep both; if you want them to join up, L11 could say "One state" only after L6 has shown the map. Do not say "Florida" for the game (it is Leonida) and do not say Bloomberg reported a cut "to Florida".
