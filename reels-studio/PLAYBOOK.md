## 1. The one paragraph strategy

Launch week is the one moment when a zero follower account can borrow the whole internet's attention, and the reference account proves it: @unseenbyneuralway had **325 followers** when its Day 3 reel reached **236,295 views in about 22.5 hours** (about 22.4K likes, 149 comments), after Day 1 at 28.7K and Day 2 at 138.8K. The same craft on non GTA topics (Diwali trading, WHOOP) got 352 and 2,725 views, so the topic and the timing unlocked distribution, not the follower count. Instagram rewards exactly this right now: a new account's reach is almost all non followers, sends per reach weigh most with that audience (Mosseri, reported), small creators' posts get tested on non followers ("breaking content"), and nearly 45% of recommended Reels are under a day old (Meta Q3 2026 earnings call). But about **404K views over three days turned into roughly 325 followers, about 0.08%**. The likely leaks: a mixed grid (Diwali, WHOOP, GTA), a name that does not say GTA, follows split with the @neuralway.ai collab account, no follow call to action anywhere, and a countdown framed to end at launch. HARD NUMBERS keeps the mechanics (first word by 0.2 s, one huge number made human scale, a rehook at the midpoint, an easy last question) and plugs every leak: one topic from post one, "GTA 6" in the name field and bio, a follow line that names tomorrow's number, a pinned start here reel, and a series that keeps counting after 19 Nov, when the biggest audience arrives. The edge in a niche full of leaks and AI fakes is trust: one verified number per reel, the source on screen, every claim labeled, and official footage used the careful way (credited, silent, short, always under heavy commentary), with AI b roll only for what no trailer shows.

### New lane: paid clipping campaigns

* **What:** campaigns that pay creators per 1,000 views on clips they post, with no follower threshold, so income can start on day one.
* **Evidence:** the @ashutoshwithclaude GTA 6 clipping reel above (23.8K likes, 3,802 comments). Tool shown: OpenShorts, https://github.com/mutonby/openshorts, which cuts long videos into shorts. Rates, terms and the campaigns themselves are not in the research file: verify each before joining.
* **Rule 1: separate account.** Never post campaign clips on HARD NUMBERS. Instagram stops recommending accounts that mostly post content they did not make (April 2026 update), and 10 or more reposts in 30 days already cost recommendations (2024 rule). One lost eligibility kills launch week reach.
* **Rule 2: rights.** Only clips the rights holder commissions. No Rockstar material in a campaign Rockstar does not run, no leaks, no streamer clips without the campaign's license.
* **Rule 3: disclosure.** Paid clips are paid partnerships: platform label plus "#ad".
* **Rule 4: it is income.** The compliance gate applies from the first cent.

### Black Friday (27 Nov) and Cyber Monday (30 Nov)

* **Gate first.** No affiliate account, link or code until the compliance gate is cleared in writing.
* **One reel:** Wed 25 Nov, "Up to 212 GB: the Black Friday buy" (section 3). The useful angle is storage and headsets for people whose console is full, not the game itself.
* **Push accessories:** headphones pay 3%; games and consoles pay 1%. Check the storage and controller category rates in Associates Central (unverified).
* **Where links live:** the bio link page only, with "As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases". TikTok bio links may not exist below 1,000 followers.
* **Disclose three ways:** spoken ("I earn a commission if you buy through my link"), on screen ("AFFILIATE LINK"), and in the caption ("#ad"), plus each platform's tool.
* **Cyber Monday:** no repost. A Story, or a recut with a new first 3 s and new numbers.
* **Never:** GTA money sellers, modded accounts, grey market keys, gambling, "free GTA 6" giveaways.
* **Expectation:** tens to low hundreds of dollars (estimate).

### Compliance gate

* **F1 student status (if it applies):** USCIS limits F1 work to authorized categories: on campus work with limits, and off campus only with CPT, OPT, STEM OPT or a hardship authorization. Creator income (revenue shares, brand deals, affiliate commissions, paid clipping) is generally treated as self employment. Unauthorized employment is a failure to maintain status (8 CFR 214.1(e)). **Turn on no payout program, affiliate account, paid deal or clipping campaign until your DSO or an immigration attorney confirms authorization.**
* **Self employment tax (if allowed to earn):** 15.3% (12.4% Social Security up to the wage base plus 2.9% Medicare) on net self employment earnings of $400 or more, on Schedule SE with Schedule C. Estimated payments if you expect to owe $1,000 or more.
* **Forms:** income is taxable with or without a form. Form 1099 NEC threshold reportedly $2,000 for payments after 31 Dec 2025; Form 1099 K reportedly over $20,000 and 200 transactions. Platforms ask for a W9 (US persons) or W8BEN (non US persons).
* **Note:** most F1 students are nonresident aliens for tax in their first 5 calendar years and generally owe no SE tax, but other rules apply (IRS Publication 519). The immigration limit applies either way.
* **Caveat:** the research could not open the IRS, USCIS or eCFR pages, so these points come from prior knowledge. Confirm with the DSO and a tax professional.
* **Tools:** ElevenLabs commercial use needs a paid plan (Starter or higher). Confirm the Higgsfield plan's commercial terms.

## 8. Open questions the creator must answer

1. **Status:** are you on an F1 visa, and do you have CPT or OPT? Resident or nonresident for tax? This decides whether any money is allowed.
2. **Where you live and your age:** TikTok Creator Rewards is country limited, every program needs 18+, and EU residents have extra AI disclosure duties.
3. **Handles:** is @hardnumbers.daily free on Instagram, TikTok and YouTube? If not, which fallback?
4. **Footage files:** can you get Rockstar's trailer clips without the TikTok watermark? If not, crop or use AI for those beats.
5. **Rockstar permission:** will you email copyright@take2games.com before monetizing reels that carry footage?
6. **The game:** will you own and play GTA 6 at launch, and do you want your own gameplay capture in the policy later?
7. **Launch hours:** can you post at 6:00 a.m. ET on Wed 18 and 12:15 a.m. ET on Thu 19, and reply for the first hour each time?
8. **Plans:** does your ElevenLabs plan include a commercial license? What do Higgsfield's terms and your credit budget allow per reel?
9. **ESPN item:** where did "A Night in Vice City" (NBA on ESPN, 7 p.m. ET, Wed 18) come from? It needs a primary source before it airs in a reel.
10. **KEYWORD deliverable:** what will you send (unlock table, source sheet), and through which official tool?
11. **Clipping lane:** do you want a separate account for paid clipping, and who runs it?
12. **Collabs and languages:** will you collab with similar sized GTA creators (more reach, split follows)? Spanish via Meta AI translations now, or later?
13. **Rechecks:** who checks the RECHECK items each morning at 7:00 ET, and who signs off the caption labels?
